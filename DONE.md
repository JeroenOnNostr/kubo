- **KUBO-063: Kid feed — "Next post" FAB as alternative to infinite scroll** — pending commit
  Adds a parent-toggleable per-kid setting ([src/hooks/useKuboFamily.ts](src/hooks/useKuboFamily.ts) — `KidSettings.nextPostButton`) that replaces infinite scroll on `/kid` with a tap-to-advance model. When enabled, the kid sees one post at a time; each FAB tap unlocks one more, scroll-cap only grows (the kid can always scroll back up, never past the most recently unlocked post). Implementation avoids any scroll-listener "fight" — [src/components/feed/KidFeedList.tsx](src/components/feed/KidFeedList.tsx) clips posts past `capAtIndex` to `display: none` and the post at the cap to a `KID_FEED_PEEK_PX` (12px) peek, so the document itself becomes shorter and the browser's end-of-page IS the scroll wall (no flicker, no snap-back, no momentum overshoot). FAB at [src/components/kid/NextPostFAB.tsx](src/components/kid/NextPostFAB.tsx) — fixed bottom-right above KuboKidBottomNav (right-4 bottom-[72px] z-50), size-14 rounded-full, `#F97316` Kubo orange, white ChevronDown, `selectionChanged()` haptic on tap, double-`rAF` before smooth-scrolling to the newly-unlocked post's top at 12px below viewport top. Resolves the scroll target via a parent-owned `postRefs` array (populated through a `ref` callback on each post wrapper in `KidFeedList`) instead of a cross-file DOM attribute query — the `data-kid-feed-item` attribute stays as a test hook only. Paging is driven by the cap (prefetch when within 2 posts of loaded edge) instead of the IntersectionObserver sentinel, which is unmounted in capped mode. Parent toggle on [src/pages/EditKidSettingsPage.tsx](src/pages/EditKidSettingsPage.tsx) right below "Show Blobbi tab". **Anti-spam cooldown (3s)**: after each tap the FAB `disabled`s itself and renders an SVG progress ring (`@keyframes kubo-next-post-cooldown` in [src/index.css](src/index.css)) that drains over the cooldown window — without this, a kid could just hammer-tap the button and defeat the whole anti-doomscroll goal. The native `disabled` attribute is what blocks the double-tap, so even browser-level rapid taps are swallowed, not just JS-guarded. **Reset on kid switch**: `useEffect([user?.pubkey])` in `KidHomePage` resets `unlockedCount` to 1 and scrolls to top when the active signer changes, so kid B doesn't inherit kid A's progress after a signer-swap. Verified: `tsc --noEmit` clean, `eslint` clean on touched files, `vite build` clean, 10 vitest cases across [src/components/feed/KidFeedList.test.tsx](src/components/feed/KidFeedList.test.tsx) (6 — cap=undefined / cap=N clipping / monotonic growth / sentinel hidden when capped / postRefs population / empty state) and [src/components/kid/NextPostFAB.test.tsx](src/components/kid/NextPostFAB.test.tsx) (4 — tap+cooldown disable/re-enable, ring render lifecycle, scroll-target resolution, no-op when target missing), Playwright check (`/tmp/kubo-066-verify.py`) confirms FAB positioning (right-gap 16px, bottom-gap 72px), z-index 50, rgb(249,115,22), toggle persists to `kubo:family` localStorage, FAB hidden when setting off, cooldown flow (disabled → ring rendered → re-enabled after 3s → re-disables on second tap), cap resets after remount.
